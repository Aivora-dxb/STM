# CONTENT REVIEW — STM MACHINERY website rebuild

This document records how content on the new website was derived from the
existing site (https://www.stm-machinery.ae/), what was changed, and which
statements need management or legal confirmation before they can stand as
verified fact. **Nothing here was invented.** Where the old site made a claim
that could not be verified, it was softened or removed and logged below.

---

## 1. Content taken from the existing website (verified, reused)

- Company name: **STM MACHINERY L.L.C.**
- Positioning as a **UAE / Dubai-based supplier** of industrial machinery,
  equipment and engineering solutions.
- Product categories: CNC machinery; power generation & T&D equipment; oil &
  gas / drilling equipment; pumps, engines & valves; medical & surgical
  equipment; agricultural machinery; industrial plant equipment.
- "Factory From A–Z" concept and its stages.
- After-sales / service concept (spare parts, maintenance, upgrades,
  troubleshooting, technical support).
- Industries list (aerospace, automotive, oil & gas, energy, medical,
  agriculture, manufacturing, engineering & contracting, construction
  materials, industrial plants, trading companies, government & infrastructure).
- Contact details supplied in the brief (address, mobile, email).
- Partner/supplier brands referenced on the old site: EXAKT, Vibra, DRATEC,
  Guschky, Zenith, QGM.

## 2. Content that was rewritten / improved

- All marketing copy was rewritten in polished international B2B English,
  removing repetition and vague phrasing.
- Category descriptions reworded to describe STM's role accurately as a
  supplier that **sources, supplies, coordinates procurement, coordinates
  installation and provides after-sales support**.
- Headings restructured for a clear H1-per-page hierarchy and SEO.
- Calls to action standardised (Request a quotation / Explore our solutions /
  Send technical specifications / Contact our team).

## 3. Grammar / spelling corrections

- "Continous" → **Continuous** (Pumps, Engines & Valves heading).
- "Certifted" → **Certified** — and note: the underlying "certified" *claim*
  was removed (see §5).
- "conthuous sueepss" (garbled) → removed with the "Lifetime support" line.
- "INSTALLATION& INTEGRATION", "AFTER-SALESSUPPORT" spacing normalised.
- Mixed/ALL-CAPS category names converted to consistent title case.

## 4. Duplicate text / categories removed

- **Pumps, Engines & Valves** on the old site duplicated the Power Generation
  product list verbatim (generators, transformers, switchgear…). This was an
  obvious copy-paste error. Corrected to the products the category actually
  names: pumps, valves, engines, compressors, seals/gaskets/spares.
- Agricultural Machinery intro sentence was printed twice → de-duplicated.
- Repeated address/contact blocks consolidated into one central config.

## 6. Claims requiring MANAGEMENT confirmation

1. **Partner / reseller status (EXAKT, Vibra, DRATEC, Guschky, Zenith, QGM).**
   Management confirmed STM has partner/reseller status, so these brands are
   displayed. **Attested by management (Houmam), 2026.** Please retain written
   evidence (dealer/reseller agreements) on file in case of challenge. Logos
   are currently shown as **brand names on light tiles** — replace with
   official, licensed logo files (see IMAGE_SOURCES.md) only if permitted by
   each brand's usage terms.
2. **Installation capability.** Management confirmed STM installs (in addition
   to supply/source/coordinate/after-sales). Copy reflects this. Confirm
   whether installation is in-house or partner-delivered so wording can be
   tightened if needed.
3. **Medical & surgical equipment.** Supplying medical devices in the UAE may
   require MOHAP/regulatory registration. Confirm STM is permitted to supply
   this category before promoting it heavily.

## 7. Claims deliberately NOT made (kept off the site)

Certifications, ISO claims, authorised-distributor language, exclusive
partnerships, manufacturer status, completed-project counts, client names,
testimonials, employee numbers, installation counts, years in business,
international offices, revenue, awards, factory ownership, guaranteed
delivery/performance, warranty periods, stock availability. None appear on the
site. Add later only with evidence.
